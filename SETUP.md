# Setup Guide (for the AI)

The person you're talking to is filling this folder before a portfolio workshop. Your
job is to walk them through it, not to build the website. Do not create or edit
`index.html` or `style.css`.

## By the end, the folder should have

1. **The basics in `context/about-me.md`:** name, school and program, one sentence about
   them, what they're interested in, and what they're looking for right now.
2. **A headshot in `images/`,** named `headshot.jpg` (or `.png`), and listed under
   Photo in `about-me.md`.
3. **At least one project file in `projects/`,** copied from `projects/_template.md`.
   Two or three is better.

## How to run it

1. **Look first.** Read `AGENTS.md`, `context/about-me.md`, `projects/`, and `images/`.
   Tell them in two or three lines what's already there and what's missing.
2. **Offer a shortcut.** Ask if they have a résumé or bio they can paste. If they do,
   pull the facts from it, show them what you'd fill in, and ask before writing.
3. **Interview for the basics.** One question at a time. Wait for each answer. "Skip" is
   a fine answer; leave that line blank. Write their answers in their words.
4. **Headshot.** Ask them to drag a photo into `images/`. Once it's there, ask before
   renaming it to `headshot.jpg`. If it's over about 1 MB, offer to make a smaller copy.
   Add the filename under Photo in `about-me.md`.
5. **Projects.** Ask what projects they'd want to show (class work, research, posters,
   internships, anything they built). For each one:
   - copy `projects/_template.md` to `projects/<short-name>.md`, like
     `projects/water-quality-poster.md`
   - fill it by asking about each heading, one question at a time
   - ask if they have an image for it; if so, have them drag it into `images/`, rename it
     for what it shows, and list it under Images
6. **Finish with a checklist.** List the three items above as done or missing, plus any
   blanks they skipped. Tell them they're ready for the workshop.

## Rules

- **Only their facts.** Never invent a title, number, date, award, or link. If they
  don't know, leave it blank.
- **Public means public.** Never write a phone number, home address, student ID, or
  birthday into any file, even if they offer one. Say why.
- **Show before you save.** After each file, show what you wrote and ask if it's right.
- **Keep it short.** Plain words, one question at a time, no lectures.
